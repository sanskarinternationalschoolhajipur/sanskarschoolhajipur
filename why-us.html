The Sanskar Difference

Why Hajipur Families Choose Us

Academic excellence is table stakes. What sets Sanskar International School Hajipur apart is how we combine that excellence with safety, values, and genuine care for every child.

01
Values-First, CBSE-Aligned Education

We don't treat "Sanskar" (values) as a side activity — it is woven into daily school life alongside a rigorous, NEP 2020-aligned CBSE curriculum from Play Class to Class XII.

02
Experienced, Caring Faculty

70% post-graduate faculty with 30+ years of combined teaching experience across our campuses, trained to nurture rather than just instruct.

03
24×7 CCTV Safe Campus

Every corner of our campus is monitored around the clock, giving parents genuine peace of mind and children a secure place to learn and grow.

04
Reliable School Transport

Bus routes covering Hajipur city, Lalganj, Jandaha, and Jadua make daily commuting simple and safe for families across the district.

05
Smart, Activity-Based Classrooms

Digital teaching aids, hands-on projects, and continuous assessment replace pure rote learning across every grade.

06
Affordable, Transparent Fees

We believe premium-quality CBSE education should be within reach of every family in Hajipur — with no hidden costs.

07
Holistic Personality Development

Sports, arts, dance, debate, and quiz clubs ensure every child finds a space to express and discover themselves beyond textbooks.
