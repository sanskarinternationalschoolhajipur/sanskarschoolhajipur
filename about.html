Sanskar International School Hajipur is a CBSE-affiliated, English-medium institution dedicated to holistic education, blending modern academic excellence with strong Indian values (Sanskar), from Play Class to Class XII.

Our story

A school built on values, growing with vision.

Founded in 2009 under the aegis of Sanskar Shikshan & Vikas Sansthan, Sanskar International School Hajipur was established with one clear purpose: to give the children of Hajipur, Vaishali and the wider Bihar region access to a genuinely modern, CBSE-aligned education without losing sight of India’s cultural and moral foundations.

What began as a modest campus on Devraj Path, Hathsarganj has grown into a trusted name across Hajipur, with a second campus serving families in Patna (Sandalpur). Every year, the school welcomes new families into a community that values curiosity, discipline, creativity and character in equal measure.

Our motto

ज्ञान दीपेन भास्वता

“Illuminated by the Lamp of Knowledge”

Not just an inscription on our crest. It reflects our belief that education should light a path, not just fill a mark-sheet.

Our mission

What we promise.

  • Deliver CBSE-aligned, NEP 2020-based academics from Play Class through Class XII.
  • Build activity-based, experiential classrooms rather than rote learning.
  • Instil strong Indian values, discipline and civic responsibility in every child.
  • Maintain a safe, 24×7 CCTV-monitored, bully-free environment for every student.
  • Keep quality education accessible and affordable for Hajipur families.

Our vision

To be the most trusted CBSE school in Vaishali district and beyond, a place where academic rigour, co-curricular excellence and rooted Indian values shape confident, future-ready citizens.
