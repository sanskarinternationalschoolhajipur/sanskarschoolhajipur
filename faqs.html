Sanskar International School Hajipur is widely regarded as one of the best CBSE schools in Hajipur, Vaishali, Bihar. It offers English medium education from Play Class to Class XII, with activity-based learning, smart classrooms, 24x7 CCTV safety, and an NEP 2020-aligned curriculum.

Yes. The school is officially affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and follows the CBSE curriculum from Play Class through Class XII, including Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Arts streams.

Play Class (age 1.5–2.5 years), Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, UKG, and Class I through Class XII, with Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Arts/Humanities streams at senior secondary level.
