Quickanswers.

Everything families ask us most, in one place. Can’t find yours? Call +91 77070 90111.

Is the school CBSE affiliated?

Yes. The school is officially CBSE-affiliated (Affiliation No. 330815), offering Play Class through Class XII with Science, Commerce and Arts streams.

Are admissions open?

Yes, for all classes at both our Hajipur and Patna campuses. Call +91 77070 90111 for the current session and dates.

Does the school provide transport?

Yes. Our bus service covers Hajipur city, Lalganj, Jandaha and Jadua.

Which classes does the school offer?

Play Class to Class XII, with Science, Commerce and Arts streams at senior secondary level.

How do you teach?

Activity-based, experiential classrooms with smart, project-driven teaching aligned to NEP 2020, so understanding comes before memorisation.

Is the campus safe?

Yes. The campus is monitored by 24×7 CCTV and we maintain a bully-free environment for every student.

Where are your campuses?

Hajipur: Near Devraj Path, Hathsarganj, Hajipur, Vaishali, Bihar 844102. Patna: No.1, Shiv Shakti Nagar Rd, Jai Mahavir Colony, Sandalpur, Patna, Bihar 800006.

When can I visit or call?

Our campus and office are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

How do I apply?

Call or email our admissions team, visit the campus, then submit the application form and documents. See the Admissions page for details.

Who manages the school?

The school is run and managed by Sanskar Shikshan & Vikas Sansthan.
