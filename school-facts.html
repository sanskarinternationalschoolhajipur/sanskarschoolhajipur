Sanskar InternationalSchool Hajipur.

The key facts about the school in one place: affiliation, classes, campuses, safety, transport and contact details.

In one paragraph

Sanskar International School Hajipur is a CBSE-affiliated, English-medium school in Hajipur, Vaishali district, Bihar, India (CBSE Affiliation No. 330815). Founded in 2009 and managed by Sanskar Shikshan & Vikas Sansthan, it teaches Play Class to Class XII, with Science, Commerce and Arts streams at senior secondary level, from two campuses: Hajipur and Sandalpur, Patna.
